Questions and answers

Is Kibblewise a vet?

No. Kibblewise gives general guidance and tells you when to see a vet. In an emergency, always call a clinic.

Which pets does it know about?

Dogs and cats for now. Rabbits are next.

Who pays for Kibblewise?

Maya does, from her own savings. We have not taken any money from investors.

What happens to my pet's information?

It stays in your account. We never sell it. See our privacy notice.
