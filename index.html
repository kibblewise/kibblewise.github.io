Your pet has a question at 2 a.m. Now you have someone to ask.

Kibblewise is a friendly AI assistant that answers pet owners' health and food questions any time of day.

Join the waitlist

Made for dog and cat people

We are building Kibblewise for dog and cat owners in Canada and the United States. Ask it if grapes are safe for your beagle, how much your new kitten should eat, or whether that cough means a trip to the vet tonight. It answers in plain words, and it tells you clearly when a question needs a real vet.

Ask anything

Food, behaviour, vaccines, the strange thing your dog just ate. Type it the way you would say it.

It knows your pet

Tell it once about breed, age, weight and allergies. Every answer after that is about your pet, not pets in general.

It knows its limits

When something sounds urgent, Kibblewise stops chatting and shows you the nearest clinic that is open now.

Where we are today

Kibblewise is a working prototype. Right now only a small test group of our friends and family uses it, and we have not launched to the public yet. So far, more than 2,300 pet owners have joined the waitlist for the first release.
