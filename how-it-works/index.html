How it works

The problem

Pet owners have hundreds of small health and food questions a year. Today they search the web, read three forums that disagree, and either panic or wait too long. Clinics are busy, and their phone lines are closed at night. In Maya's old clinic, about a third of all phone calls were questions that did not need an appointment at all.

Our solution

Kibblewise is a chat assistant on your phone. You set up a short profile for each pet, then you ask questions in your own words. Each answer comes from a large language model that is limited to a library of answers our advising veterinarian has checked.

1. Ask

"My lab ate a sock an hour ago, what now?"

2. Get a clear answer

Every answer starts with one of three signs: fine at home, call your vet today, or go now.

3. Keep a diary

Kibblewise remembers symptoms and meals, so you can show your vet what happened.

The prototype runs on iPhone and Android for our test group. The public release comes after our first funding round.
