What we built the prototype with

By Maya Okafor, September 2026

People ask how one person built a working app in six months. The honest answer: I kept it small and used good tools.

Everything runs on Google Cloud. The chat service, the answer library and the database all live there. Jonah does all of the design work in Figma, and we watch how our test group uses the app with PostHog, so we can see where people get stuck.

All in, our tools cost about $300 a month. Most of that is cloud.
